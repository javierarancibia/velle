## Summary

One 30-second video of a woman tearing up when she receives the lamp drove 815,304 of LuminWorks' 942,208 EU reach (87%). An emotional gift-reaction video should lead the Velle Christmas campaign.

- **Format:** 7 of the 10 ads are video and hold 96% of EU reach. The 3 image ads are all Black Friday price offers.
- **Messages:** three themes repeat. The gift reaction ("she started tearing up") is in 5 ads and gets 91% of reach. Feeling Jesus's presence in a nightly prayer routine is in 2 ads (5%). Black Friday urgency with 55% off and Buy 2 Get 1 Free is in 3 ads (4%).
- **Spain:** every ad was in English and targeted 12 EU countries. Spain got about 69,000 reach (7%). Nobody spoke to Spanish buyers in Spanish, so Velle has room there.
- **Impressions:** Meta does not publish impressions for these ads. EU reach (accounts that saw the ad at least once) is the closest public figure. The library sorts by impressions, so the ranking order is still a useful signal.
- **For Velle:** run a Spanish-language gift-reaction video as the main ad. Put the most budget into 2–20 December, your best month in 2025 (9,80 € per sale), with Black Friday (27 Nov) and Reyes (6 Jan) as smaller pushes. Use "Paga 2 y recibe 3" as the offer.

Reach is not sales. The top ad also ran for about 18 months, which inflates its total.

## Image vs video

Video won by volume because of one breakout ad. Leave that ad out and the typical video and the typical image reached about the same number of people, 14,330 vs 13,641 per ad.

| Format | Ads | EU reach | Share of reach | Avg reach per ad | Avg without ad #1 | Reach aged 35–64 | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Video (20–30 s) | 7 | 901,285 | 96% | 128,755 | 14,330 | 49% (without #1) | Emotion: reaction, prayer routine |
| Image (static) | 3 | 40,923 | 4% | 13,641 | 13,641 | 59% | Offer: 55% off, Buy 2 Get 1 Free |

- **Video is for discovery.** All four Valentine reaction videos used identical copy. They look like edits of one shoot tested against each other, and one of them took almost all the spend.
- **Images are for the offer.** All three images are Black Friday price ads. They reached an older group (35–64), which is likely closer to the person buying a 45 € gift.
- **Watch the delivery skew.** Ad #1 reached 67% men and 58% people aged 18–34. Its twin (#4) reached mostly people aged 35–64. Broad targeting can chase cheap reach, so judge ads in Ads Manager by purchases, not reach.
- **Untested formats:** no carousel, no UGC testimonial, and no Spanish- or local-language versions.

## What to copy, what to improve

Copy the gift-reaction structure and the light-on moment. Then beat LuminWorks where they were weak in Spain: language, local gift dates, and product proof.

**Copy**

- **The reaction hook:** open on the person receiving the frame, not on the product. "Se emocionó al ver a Jesús iluminarse" is the Spanish equivalent of the winning line.
- **Light off, then light on in the first 3 seconds.** Velle already has the footage (encendido.mp4, luz-apagada-encendida.webp).
- **Several edits of one shoot,** let Meta pick a winner, then put the budget on it.
- **Images only for the offer,** shown late in the funnel.

**Improve**

- **Speak native Spanish.** LuminWorks ran English copy in Spain. Use "tú" and Spanish faith words: "bendición", "que te acompañe", "para el cuarto del niño", "un regalo para la abuela".
- **Use Spanish gift dates.** In Spain the big gift moment is Reyes Magos (6 Jan), not only 25 Dec. LuminWorks never mentioned either.
- **Show who it is for.** The ads were generic ("loved ones"). Velle has lifestyle images for grandmother, child, young person, mother and daughter, and couple. Make one ad for each.
- **Proof that matters in Spain:** hand-painted, free shipping in Spain, 14-day returns, free replacement if damaged, Salmo 73:23. LuminWorks only used a customer count.
- **Don't fake discounts.** LuminWorks ran "ends tonight" ads for months. "Paga 2 y recibe 3" (3 for 89,80 € instead of 134,70 €) is a real, simple offer.

**Limits of this sample:** 10 ads, no spend, impressions, clicks or sales data. EU reach is Meta's estimate, and the top ad ran for 18 months, so a high total does not prove it was profitable.

### Rules that protect the profit

- **Stop when stock runs out.** If you sell out early, the rest of the budget is never spent.
- **Stop-loss:** if the 7-day cost per sale goes above 30 €, cut prospecting by half and keep only retargeting.
- **One ad per ad set in the test.** Each has its own 5 € a day, so Meta has to spend on every ad instead of starving the challengers.
- **Pause a challenger** that spends 36 € (2× the 18 € target) with no sale.
- **Scale a winner** by 20% every 48 hours while its cost per sale stays under 18 €.
- **Replace the old video as soon as a new one wins.** Your compilation is the benchmark, not the plan for all of December.
- **Leftover stock is fine.** Frames not sold by 6 Jan can sell for Día de la Madre (2 May 2027). Don't cut prices just to clear them.

Delivery is not in the 4 €. The courier costs about 6–8 € per order and shipping is free for the customer, so it costs about 1.000–1.330 € over 166 orders (the figures above use 7 €). At 30 € per sale the plan only just breaks even, so the 30 € stop-loss matters.
