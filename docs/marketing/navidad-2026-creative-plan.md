## Summary

The Christmas campaign needs 6 different concepts, not 13 versions of one. Today almost every finished video says the same thing ("la fe ilumina tu hogar") over the same 8 AI clips, so Meta treats them as one ad.

- **Avatar** = who buys and why (grandchild buying for a grandmother, a parent buying for a child, someone buying for themselves).
- **Angle** = the idea that makes them buy (her reaction, the light reveal, the nightly prayer, remembering someone, proof the product is real, the offer).
- **Concept** = one avatar + one angle, with its own footage, first frame and words. Each concept gets its own ad set in Meta, so each one gets money and a fair test.
- **Hook variants** = 2 or 3 versions of the same video that change only the first 3 seconds. They go inside the concept's ad set and compete with each other.

Meta groups ads that look alike and gives the budget to just one of them. A new concept only counts if the first frame, the people, the setting and the message are all different.

**What to do first:** fix the text on the 4 ads you already have, re-edit the ASMR video, and produce the 2 story and 2 montage videos by 12 November.

## Angles

Six angles. Each one must look different in the first second, not just use different words.

| ID | Angle | The idea in one line | What the first second shows | Funnel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R | Gift reaction | "Look how she reacted when she opened it" | A face close-up: tears, a smile, a hug | Prospecting |
| L | Light reveal | "Off, a child walks alone. On, Jesus holds his hand" | The frame switching on, filling the screen | Prospecting and retargeting |
| N | Night presence | "Every night, before sleeping, He is here" | A dark bedroom, a hand switching it on | Prospecting |
| M | Remembrance | "For those who are no longer at the table" | An empty chair or an old photo at Christmas | Prospecting |
| P | Proof | "This is exactly how it arrives, and what it really looks like" | Real hands, a real box, daylight, phone camera | Retargeting, and prospecting for doubters |
| O | Offer and deadline | "3 for 89,80 €, arrives for Nochebuena" | Three lit frames, or a big date | Retargeting |

The same light-on moment appears in every angle, because it is the product's strongest feature. What changes is who is in the scene and why it matters to them.

## Video ads

The new videos come in two formats, built from the clips produced so far (9 Nov). **Story** videos follow one gift from the hand-over to the lit frame. **Montage** videos cut 6–8 moments of different people with the frame, about 2 s each, the format most US frame brands run. Both keep the same rules: light on by second 8, an end card with the offer, and all text burned in, in Spanish, inside the middle 66% of a 9:16 frame.

## Rules for every ad

- **A different first frame per concept.** No two ad sets may open on the same clip, person or room. Inside one ad set the hook variants may share the body.
- **Light on by second 8,** and in the first 3 seconds when the angle is L.
- **Real reviews only.** Use only Valeria, Leonor, Teresa and José María, or new customers who give permission, always with their real name and city.
- **No invented urgency.** No "últimas unidades", "pocas unidades" or "solo por tiempo limitado" while you have stock and the offer has no end date. Black Friday (27–30 Nov) and the delivery cut-offs are real dates and can be used.
- **The verse reads "Salmo 73:23".** The AI clips show "Psalm 73:23" on the lit frame. Regenerate those scenes, or avoid close-ups where it can be read.
- **Only claims the website makes:** 17,5 × 23,5 cm, 3 light tones, dimmer on the cable, free shipping in Spain, delivery in 24–72 h, 14-day returns, free replacement if it arrives damaged.
- **Text inside the safe zone:** nothing in the top 14% or the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame. The current "Velle" logo in the bottom-right corner sits under the Reels buttons; move it higher.
- **Spanish "tú",** burned-in captions, no watermarks, no other brand's footage.
