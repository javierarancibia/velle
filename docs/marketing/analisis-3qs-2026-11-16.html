Creatives and reach

  • Hook: 3-second plays 29,7 % of impressions 🟡 — just under the 30 % line.
  • Retention: 4 s average 🟡 (Abuela 1A and Abuelo 2A 5 s; Control V0 and 6C 3 s).
  • Frequency 1,43 🟢 — no fatigue.
  • CPM 10,15 € — above every past month (6,77–9,81 €): Christmas auction pressure.
  • Placements: Facebook 39 € (66 clicks) and Instagram 49 € (67 clicks) split evenly; Audience Network only 0,60 €. Junk placements are not the problem.

Why nobody presses "Comprar" — what the page shows

  • Cookie banner covers the bottom third of the phone screen on arrival and stays fixed while scrolling, over the product title, the price and the pack selector, until the visitor answers it. Every ad visitor is a first-time visitor, so every one sees it.
  • Otherwise the page is in good shape: clear packs (44,90 / 79,90 / 89,80 €), "Comprar ahora" button, payment logos, guarantees, reviews. "Pintado a mano" is gone from the page.
  • Card only. Past campaigns (92 sales, 19,20 € each) ran on the old store with cash on delivery. The plan set a 7-day card-only test with a decision on day 7 (20 Nov).

Why retargeting spends nothing

  • All 6 Evaluación ads are approved and active, but the ad set has 0 impressions.
  • Its audiences are tiny: web visitors 30 days = 20 people (the pixel only counts visitors who accept cookies); Facebook Page engagers 30 days ≈ 1.000. Too small for Meta to deliver a purchase-optimised ad set.

Algorithm signal

Meta optimises for Purchase but has received no real purchase since launch, and sees only consenting visitors. Without signal it cannot learn whom to show the ads to.

Copy issue

The Control ad (V0, October creative) still says "está pintado a mano" — removed from the site on 12 Nov because the cuadro is not hand-painted. A misleading product claim in a paid ad is a consumer-law and Meta-policy risk.
